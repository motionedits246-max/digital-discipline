The Method

A calmer sequence for your day.

Traditional screen-time apps shame you with notifications or lock your machine with rigid blocks. Digital Discipline takes a local-first, human approach: understand what is happening, decide what matters, and build durable focus habits.

01

See your day

The Rust background agent quietly observes foreground executables and idle intervals. Sessions are recorded into local SQLite with bounded timelines. No window titles, keystrokes, or clipboard data are ever collected.

What Digital Discipline Tracks

  • Executable basename: e.g. code.exe, obsidian.exe (no file paths)
  • Active interval & idle: Duration of user activity
  • Opted-in browser domains: Only if browser companion is paired
  • Qualified video playback: Visible watch time on YouTube Shorts & Reels
  • User categories & goals: Custom taxonomies you configure

What Is NEVER Collected

  • NO window titles: Document names, chat partner names, or URLs
  • NO keystrokes or clipboard: Never logs typing or copied data
  • NO screenshots or screen capture: Zero visual surveillance
  • NO cloud telemetry: Zero analytics or pingbacks to developers
  • NO third-party ads: Completely free and open-source

Honest Technical Disclosures

Unencrypted local SQLite: The activity database is stored in your Windows user profile without disk encryption. If an attacker has filesystem access to your PC, they can inspect the SQLite file.
Self-directed intervention: Native app interventions are warning prompts, not tamperproof kernel blockers.
Development signature: Releases are signed with a development certificate; Windows SmartScreen will prompt for confirmation.

Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers about security, system impact, and privacy choices.

Does Digital Discipline require an account or internet connection?

No. Digital Discipline requires zero accounts, logins, or cloud infrastructure. All tracking and session logic runs completely offline on your PC. The only time an external network connection is made is if you explicitly configure your own API key for the AI Coach or approve a remote category lookup.

How is video observation different from tracking browser history?

Standard browser trackers record every full URL you visit. Digital Discipline's companion only observes domain names and checks visible playback progression on short-form video players (YouTube Shorts, regular YouTube videos, and Instagram Reels). It distinguishes between an accidental 3-second link click and an active 30-minute watch streak, without storing video titles or transcripts.

Why does Windows SmartScreen display a warning on install?

Commercial Windows code-signing certificates cost hundreds of dollars annually and require corporate registration. Because this is an independent open-source project, binaries are signed with a development certificate. To install, click "More info" and then "Run anyway". You can verify the exact SHA-256 checksum against GitHub Actions build records before running.

Can Digital Discipline permanently lock me out of my computer?

No. Distraction restrictions are intentionally bounded. Browser restriction rules use 3-minute self-expiring leases—if the desktop app closes or crashes, browser rules immediately expire. Additionally, a 30-minute emergency bypass is always accessible with one click in the focus interface.

How do the optional AI features work?

AI classification and coaching are strictly Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK). Supported providers include Ollama (which runs completely offline on local loopback), OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, and Groq. Keys are encrypted in Windows DPAPI and are never written to unencrypted storage. Each remote classification request requires explicit manual confirmation.

How do I completely remove Digital Discipline and its data?

Uninstall Digital Discipline normally through Windows Settings → Installed Apps. Because user data belongs to you, Windows uninstallation leaves your local SQLite database intact. To completely erase all recorded history and bearer tokens, delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\DigitalDiscipline.
